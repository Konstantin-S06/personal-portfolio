# Kenney Modular Dungeon Kit: notes

Source: `public/models/kit` (CC0). Measurements come from `docs/kit-inventory.json`, generated by `scripts/inspect-kit.mjs`.

## Grid cell size: 4 units

- Corridors are 4×4 (one cell), wide corridors 8×8 (two cells), small rooms 12×12 (three), wide rooms 20×12 (five by three), large rooms 20×20 (five by five). Every footprint is a multiple of 4.
- Stairs are 8.4 deep (two cells plus 0.2 margin each side).
- Gates are 4.4 wide: a 4-unit opening with 0.2 overhang per side.
- Wall height is 4.05 (about one cell), with a few pieces at 4.23 or 4.5.
- Grid lines sit at x, z = ±2, ±6, … (cell centres at 0, ±4, ±8). Corridor and room footprints are centred on these centres.

Note that walls are 2.0 units deep (half a cell), not thin.

## Conventions

- **Units:** Y is up. Floor top is y = 0; walls and pieces rise from y = 0.
- **Pivot:** the model origin, which is at y = 0 for every piece.
- **Default facing:** walls, half-walls, wall tops, gates, and doorways are 4 (or 4.4) wide along X and thin along Z. Their long axis runs along X and their normal is ±Z. The pivot is on the +Z face, so the body extends toward −Z. Which of the two faces is the visible side needs a visual check (not yet done).
- **Corridors and rooms:** symmetric footprints, so no facing is defined by the bounds.
- **Stairs:** 4.4 wide in X and 8.4 long in Z, with the pivot at x = 0, z = 0, which is 2.2 units in from the +Z end. Which end is the top is not determined by the bounds and needs a visual check.

## Piece classification

Pivot column: `center` = middle of footprint, `edge` = middle of one side, `corner` = a corner, `off-center` = neither. Size is X × Y × Z in units.
